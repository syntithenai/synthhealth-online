SynthFit

Guided gym training for you, and a coaching dashboard for your personal trainer. SynthFit is in testing and will open on the web and Android soon.

What’s coming

  • A workout player for the whole gym floor

    Around 190 real exercises across machines, free weights, kettlebells, cables, cardio and mobility, with step-by-step images, spoken cues, sets, reps and rest timers.

  • Routines and programs

    Levelled sessions and multi-week programs, plus a builder for your own routines, with progress tracking and streaks.

  • Coaching for personal trainers

    Assign programs, see who trained and how hard, and post class times. Clients train free; trainers start with one client free.

  • Food diary

    Log meals with barcode and photo scanning and keep an eye on macros, shared with your trainer only if you choose.

  • Wearables

    Live heart rate from Bluetooth straps and Wear OS watches, Health Connect sync on Android, and safety alerts during a session.

  • Your data stays yours

    Works offline. Optional Google sign-in syncs to a folder in your own Google Drive.
